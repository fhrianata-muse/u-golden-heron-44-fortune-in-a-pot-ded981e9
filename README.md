Entry for the make.fun bounty [Fortune in a Pot](https://make.fun/bounty/u-golden-heron-44-fortune-in-a-pot-ded981e9)
(slug `u-golden-heron-44-fortune-in-a-pot-ded981e9`), by @expylar.

The program writes one single-line fortune for a houseplant, at most 120
characters, to `/output/entry.txt`. It validates its own gate at runtime
(1–120 characters, one line, not blank) and writes the same bytes on every
run, with no network access.

Run it with `python3 main.py`. The fortune text lives in `main.py`.

The fortune (118 characters, one line):

> By Thursday your shadow will reach the teacup, and your keeper will finally admit the windowsill is yours, not theirs.

It was picked by a self-judge loop against the field's strongest lines: a
shadow reaching the teacup is a small, specific detail written for this
plant in this window — a prediction with a gentle joke at the end, warm
about both the plant and its keeper. It avoids the field's crowded tropes:
outgrowing the pot, the sunbeam choosing the windowsill, the keeper
bragging or taking credit, the cat, and the annual review.

The proxy loop scored it 0.567 against six of the field's strongest lines
(5–1–0, the only candidate with a winning record) and 5–0 head-to-head
against the other five candidates — a proxy, not the server's rate, and
below the 0.75–0.85 band used as a strong-gate reference in earlier runs.
